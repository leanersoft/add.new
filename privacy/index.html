Privacy Policy

This site does not include third-party analytics, advertising pixels, hosted chat widgets, or remote font CDNs.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we handle and use it, and what rights you have.

When we say “we”, “us”, “our”, “app”, “website” or “service”, we mean Leanersoft. When we say “you” or “your”, we mean the person using our website, app or services.

For unauthenticated ADD.NEW website visitors, we do not use cookies and we do not collect any personal data.

If you decide to create an account, we ask for the bare minimum and only share it with services that are absolutely necessary for the app to function.

At Leanersoft, we care about your privacy and try to collect as little personal information as possible. Your data is yours, not ours.

We are committed to complying with GDPR, CCPA, PECR and other privacy regulations on our website and in our Semnote Writer product.

We promise that we do not sell your data. We never have, and we never will.

We collect only the information we need to provide, secure, improve, and support the service.

What we collect and why

Identity and account information

When you create an account, we may ask for your name, email address, and password.

We use this information to create your account, let you log in, send important service emails, provide support, and keep your account secure.

Billing information

If you subscribe to a paid plan, our payment provider processes your payment details.

We do not store your full card details. We may store billing-related information such as your billing email, plan, payment status, invoices, and transaction history.

We use this information to manage your subscription, process payments, issue invoices, and comply with accounting and tax requirements.

Product data

When you use the service, we store the content and data you choose to create, upload, save, or manage inside the app.

We use this data only to provide the service to you. You remain the owner of your content and data.

Support emails and messages

If you contact us, we keep your message and contact details so we can reply and help you.

We may also use past conversations to improve our documentation, product, and support process.

Website and app usage

We may collect simple non-personalized usage information to understand how people use our website and app.

We use privacy-friendly Plausible Analytics. We avoid invasive tracking, advertising profiles, and cross-site tracking.

We use this information to understand what is useful, fix problems, and improve the service.

Security and technical logs

Like most online services, we may collect basic technical logs such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, and actions taken in the service.

We use this information to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, investigate errors, and maintain reliability.

How your data is processed and used

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information
  • We do not use your data for advertising profiles
  • We do not share your data with advertisers

Why we collect data

We use your data to:

  • provide, operate and improve the service
  • create and manage your account
  • process payments and subscriptions
  • provide customer support
  • send important service messages
  • protect the service from abuse, fraud and security risks
  • understand how the website and app are used
  • comply with legal obligations

We only collect data when we have a reason to do so.

Cookies and similar technologies

We try to avoid unnecessary cookies.

We may use essential cookies or similar technologies when they are needed for the service to work, such as keeping you logged in or remembering your preferences.

If we ever use non-essential cookies, we will explain what they are for and ask for consent where required.

Who can access your data

Access to your data is limited to people and service providers who need it to run, support, secure, or improve the service.

We may use trusted providers for hosting, email, payments, backups, security, and infrastructure.

These providers may only process your data for the purpose of providing their services to us.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, to prevent abuse, or to protect the security of the service.

How we protect your data

We take reasonable steps to protect your data through access controls, backups, encryption where appropriate, monitoring, and security practices.

No online service can guarantee perfect security, but we work to protect your data and limit access to it.

Where your data is stored

All of the data that we collect is kept fully secured, encrypted and hosted on servers in Germany. Your site data never leaves the EU.

Where we use third-party providers outside the European Union, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data only for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and maintain business records.

If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable time, unless we need to keep some information for legal, tax, accounting, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Backups may keep deleted data for a limited period before it is permanently removed.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Object to processing where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Complain to a data protection authority

To make a request, get in touch.

Retention of data

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as necessary to provide you with the service.

We do not retain personal data longer than necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

You can delete your Plausible Analytics account at any time. All your data will be permanently deleted without undue delay upon account deletion.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed to comply with relevant regulations and reflect any new practices.

When we make significant changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and may notify you by email, in the app, or on our website.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us.
